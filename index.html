How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

This free mortgage calculator helps you estimate your monthly repayment based on loan amount, interest rate (APR), and loan term. It is designed for home buyers, refinancing decisions, or anyone planning long-term housing costs.

Step-by-Step

  • 1. Enter the loan amount — e.g. $450,000
  • 2. Enter the annual interest rate — e.g. 5.25%
  • 3. Choose the loan term — e.g. 30 years
  • 4. Click “Calculate Repayments” to view your monthly costs

Tips to Reduce Your Mortgage Cost

✅ Compare rates from multiple lenders — even a 0.5% difference can save tens of thousands in interest.

✅ Make extra principal payments when possible — this reduces total interest significantly.

✅ Shorter loan terms (e.g. 15 years) have higher monthly payments but much lower lifetime interest.

Fixed vs. Variable (Adjustable) Rate

Fixed rate: Stable monthly payment, safer for long-term budgeting.
Variable/adjustable rate: Starts cheaper, but may increase over time depending on market conditions.
