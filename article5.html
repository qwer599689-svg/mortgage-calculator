What Is Mortgage Pre-Approval?

Mortgage pre-approval is an essential step in the home-buying process. It shows sellers you're a serious buyer and gives you clarity on how much you can borrow.

Why It Matters

In competitive markets, homes can sell quickly. Pre-approval gives you an edge over other buyers who haven’t prepared their finances.

What You Need

  • Proof of income (pay slips or tax returns)
  • Credit history and report
  • Debt-to-income ratio
  • Bank statements

How Long Does It Last?

Most pre-approvals are valid for 60 to 90 days. If your situation changes (e.g., job change or new debts), you may need to reapply.

Next Steps

After pre-approval, you can start house hunting with confidence. When you find a property, you can move quickly with a formal mortgage application.

Getting pre-approved takes some paperwork, but it saves you time and stress in the long run.
